# AI Application Engineering

A book that teaches AI application engineering through concrete cases. Each
chapter starts from one application, breaks one simplifying assumption, and
compares two ways to build it, with their failure and recovery behavior.

Start with the [introduction](book/introduction.md), or go straight to a
chapter. The [table of contents](book/table-of-contents.md) summarizes each
case in three lines.

## Status

All chapters are drafts. The fixtures, labs, and comparisons they describe
have not been run, and each chapter says so. Numbers are illustrative unless
labeled *measured*; company references link to the original articles and
support only what those articles say.

[Tone of voice](TONE.md) and [writing instructions](AGENTS.md) describe how
the chapters are written.
